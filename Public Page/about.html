🍃 Who We Are

About SwachhLens

Technology for Cleaner, Healthier and More Sustainable Cities

SwachhLens is an initiative under the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, Government of India, to empower urban local bodies with AI-driven tools for efficient waste management, cleaner environments and healthier communities.

Our Story

SwachhLens was envisioned to bridge the gap between technology and on-ground waste management. With the growing urban population and increasing waste generation, there was a need for data-driven, scalable and easy-to-use solutions for municipalities. SwachhLens brings together innovation, governance and citizen participation to create cleaner, greener and more liveable cities.
